# 🎭⚔️ Archetypes & Buffs — Why *Who* You Marry Actually Matters

In vanilla Skyrim, marrying a Jarl's court wizard, a Fearless Companions shield-sister, or a guy who chops firewood for a living gives you the exact same reward: a homemade pie and 100 gold a day. 🥧

In **M.A.R.A.S**, every romantic partner has an actual identity! Every NPC is automatically assigned a **3-part Archetype** based on their life in Skyrim, their skills, and even how fancy their clothes are:

1. 🏰 **Social Class** — Where they stand on Skyrim's social ladder *(grants **Permanent Household Buffs** & shapes how hard they are to impress)*.
2. ⚔️ **Skill Type** — What they're actually good at *(grants **Follower Combat Buffs** on the road & determines what they sell when living in your home)*.
3. 💖 **Temperament** — Their emotional personality *(shapes **Affection**, **Relationship Drama**, and **SkyrimNet AI personalities**)*.

## 1. Social Class — Where They Sit on the Ladder 🏰🧹

When M.A.R.A.S looks at an NPC, it checks their factions, job, and even whether they're wearing rich jewelry and fine clothes (`ClothingRich` counts—fake it 'til you make it! 💅) to place them into one of **8 Social Classes**:

| Social Class | Who Fits Here? | What It Means for You |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🗡️ **Outcast** | Bandits, fugitives, necromancers, folks living outside the law | Streetwise, dangerous, and surprisingly good at teaching you where to stab. |
| 🥣 **Poverty** | Beggars, struggling laborers, down-on-their-luck survivors | Tough as nails; they know how to keep going when stamina runs low. |
| 🔨 **Working** | Farmers, miners, guards, inn hands, everyday townsfolk | Strong backs and practical habits—say goodbye to being over-encumbered! |
| 🏪 **Middle** | Shopkeepers, innkeepers, skilled townsfolk with a cozy life | Stable home life that helps you learn skills faster. |
| 💰 **Wealthy** | Rich merchants, landowners, anyone dressed to the nines | Business connections that boost your selling prices across Skyrim. |
| 🙏 **Religious** | Priests, priestesses, temple healers, devoted acolytes | Divine protection that helps shrug off enemy spells. |
| 🍷 **Nobles** | Court officials, noble families, high-society elites | Aristocratic clout that gets you discounts at every shop. |
| 👑 **Rulers** | Jarls, Thanes, military commanders, high leaders | Political authority that sharpens your own silver tongue. |

> [!NOTE]
> **High Society Has High Standards!**
> Social Class doesn't just decide your buff—it also sets the bar when you propose marriage! While a **Working** or **Poverty** partner mostly cares that they like you, **Nobles**, **Rulers**, and the **Wealthy** will expect you to own property, carry serious gold, or be a famous hero before they say *"I do."*

## 2. Skill Type — What They Actually *Do* ⚔️🏹🔮

Next, M.A.R.A.S checks the NPC's combat class and their highest skills to assign one of **6 Skill Types**:

- ⚔️ **Warrior** — Experts in One-Handed, Two-Handed, Block, or Heavy Armor (soldiers, housecarls, mercenaries).
- 🔮 **Mage** — Masters of Destruction, Restoration, Conjuration, Illusion, or Alteration (court wizards, priests, spellswords).
- 🏹 **Ranger** — Sharpshooters whose top talent is Archery (hunters, scouts, woodland archers).
- 🗡️ **Rogue** — Masters of Sneak, Lockpicking, and Pickpocket (thieves, assassins, nightblades).
- ⚒️ **Craftsman** — Masters of Smithing, Alchemy, and Enchanting (blacksmiths, apothecaries, enchanters).
- 🗣️ **Orator** — Silver-tongued experts whose standout talent is Speech (bards, merchants, diplomats).

*(Fun fact: If an NPC's highest skill is **Light Armor**, M.A.R.A.S checks whether they're better with a bow or with lockpicks/pickpocketing to decide if they're a **Ranger** or a **Rogue**!)*

### 🛒 Bonus Perks of Skill Type: Home Shopkeepers & Guild Respect!
Your spouse's Skill Type does **three** cool things in gameplay:
1. **Follower Combat Buffs**: Gives you a combat bonus whenever they travel with you as an active follower.
2. **Specialized Home Merchant**: When a spouse moves into one of your registered **Player Houses**, they set up a small merchant stall matching their Skill Type! Marry a **Mage** and buy spell tomes and soul gems at home; marry a **Craftsman** for ingots and smithing gear; marry a **Ranger** to restock arrows before heading out!
3. **Guild Bonding**: When you're courting an NPC, sharing their specialty helps win them over—**Warriors** respect the Companions, **Mages** respect the College of Winterhold, and **Rogues** respect the Thieves Guild!

## 3. Temperament — Their Emotional Personality 😇😈

Unless an NPC has a custom personality override, their **Temperament** is born from the combination of their **Social Class** and **Skill Type**:

| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 🗡️ **Outcast** | 🐺 Independent | 👀 Jealous | 👀 Jealous | 🌱 Humble | 🐺 Independent | ❤️ Romantic |
| 🥣 **Poverty** | 🌱 Humble | ❤️ Romantic | 👀 Jealous | 🐺 Independent | 👑 Proud | ❤️ Romantic |
| 🔨 **Working** | 👑 Proud | 🌱 Humble | ❤️ Romantic | 🐺 Independent | 🐺 Independent | 👑 Proud |

| 🙏 **Religious** | 🐺 Independent | 🌱 Humble | ❤️ Romantic | 👀 Jealous | 🌱 Humble | 👑 Proud |

| 👑 **Rulers** | 👑 Proud | 🐺 Independent | 🌱 Humble | ❤️ Romantic | 👀 Jealous | 🐺 Independent |

### What Does Temperament Actually Do?
Temperament is the heartbeat of how your partner behaves in a relationship:
- ❤️ **Romantic** — Loves gifts, intimacy, and sleeping side-by-side (`1.5x` bonus!), gets heartbroken if ignored for days (`-4`/day decay), and stays fiercely loyal.
- 👀 **Jealous** — Craves reassurance and intimacy, **hates** open relationships (`-35` Affection just for asking!), and will lose their mind if they catch you with someone else.
- 👑 **Proud** — Cares deeply about status! Promoting them to **Lead Spouse** makes their day (`+24`), while demoting them down the household hierarchy is a personal insult (`-24`).
- 🌱 **Humble** — Easygoing, patient when you're away (`-2`/day decay), and doesn't throw a fit over household rank changes.
- 🐺 **Independent** — Low-maintenance lone wolves who barely mind when you disappear into Blackreach for a week (`-1`/day decay) and are thrilled if you suggest an **Open Relationship** (`+15` Affection)—though they're also the most likely to pursue their own side romances if left unattended!

## 6. Buff Math Made Simple: Monogamy vs. Harem & Mood Scaling 📈🧮

Not every marriage gives the exact same numbers every day. Your actual bonus is scaled by **how happy your spouse is** and **how your household is structured**:

### Step 1: The Mood Multiplier (Applies to *All* Buffs)
Your spouse's current **Affection Mood Tier** multiplies both their Permanent Household Buff and their Follower Combat Buff:
- 😀 **Happy (`75–100`)**: **`1.25x`** *(+25% extra strength!)*
- 🙂 **Content (`50–74`)**: **`1.00x`** *(Full normal strength)*
- 😕 **Troubled (`25–49`)**: **`0.25x`** *(Slashed by 75%—they are not feeling supportive right now)*
- 💔 **Estranged (`0–24`)**: **`0.00x`** *(Zero! You get nothing until you fix your marriage)*

### Step 2: Monogamy Bonus vs. Polygamy Rank (Permanent Buffs Only)
For **Permanent Household Buffs** (and daily store gold!), M.A.R.A.S balances faithful monogamy against sprawling multi-spouse households:

- 💍 **The Monogamy Devotion Bonus (`2.0x`!)**:
  If you are married to **only one spouse**, their Permanent Household Buff is **DOUBLED (`2.0x`)**!
  *Example*: One **Happy (`1.25x`) Working** spouse gives you `30 × 2.0 × 1.25 =` **`+75 Carry Weight`** all by themselves!

- 👑 **Polygamy Household Ranks (2 or More Spouses)**:
  Once you marry a second spouse, the Monogamy Bonus turns off and your spouses contribute based on their **Spouse Hierarchy Rank**:
  - **Lead Spouse (1st Rank)**: **`1.00x`**
  - **Second Spouse (2nd Rank)**: **`0.50x`**
  - **Third Spouse (3rd Rank)**: **`0.25x`**
  - **Fourth+ Spouses (4th, 5th, 10th...)**: **`0.10x` each**

- 🚶 **What About Follower Combat Buffs?**
  Follower buffs **ignore household rank**! Whether your follower spouse is Rank 1 or Rank 12, if they are fighting beside you in a dungeon, you get their full **`1.0x` × Mood Multiplier** combat buff.
  *(Plus, if you use a multi-follower mod and bring **multiple** spouses along at the same time, all of their Follower Buffs stack!)*

## 7. Viewing & Customizing Archetypes in the Dashboard 🖥️🔧

You never have to guess or calculate these numbers in your head:

1. **See Your Live Buffs**:
   Press **`Alt + B`** (default) to open the **[M.A.R.A.S Dashboard](UI_DASHBOARD_GUIDE.md)** and head to **My Household $\rightarrow$ Household Buffs**. You'll see all 14 buffs, your exact active percentages, which spouses are contributing right now, and even which spouses sitting at home *could* give you a Follower Buff if you invite them along!
2. **Change Any NPC's Archetype Anytime**:
   Think your favorite modded follower should be a **Noble Mage** instead of a **Working Warrior**? Click on any NPC in the Dashboard to open their **Detail Page** and use the dropdowns to change their **Social Class**, **Skill Type**, or **Temperament** on the fly—your buffs update immediately! 🎉
